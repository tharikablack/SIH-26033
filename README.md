## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Proposed Solution
We propose a FarmDirect – Direct Farmer to Consumer Marketplace, a digital platform that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, hotels and supermarkets without depending on multiple intermediaries.

The platform allows farmers to register, upload their agricultural products, set their own prices and receive orders directly from buyers.

It includes AI-based fair price suggestions, live market price updates, location-based farmer discovery, online payments and delivery management.

This solution helps farmers earn better profits, reduces product prices for consumers, minimizes food wastage and creates a transparent agricultural marketplace.

## Technical Approach
Technical Approach
1. Frontend Development: Use HTML, CSS and JavaScript to develop a user-friendly website for farmers and consumers.

2. Backend Development: Use Python and Django to manage user registration, product listings, orders and transactions.

3. Database Management: Use MySQL or SQLite to store farmer details, product information, prices and order history.

4. AI-Based Price Prediction: Implement machine learning to analyse market prices and suggest fair prices for agricultural products.

5. Location-Based Services: Integrate Google Maps API to connect nearby farmers with consumers.

6. Order Management: Develop a system to manage product availability, customer orders and delivery status.

7. Payment Integration: Integrate a secure digital payment gateway for online transactions.

8. Testing and Deployment: Test the application for security, performance and usability before deploying it online.

Expected Outcome: A reliable digital marketplace that reduces intermediaries, improves farmers' earnings and provides affordable fresh products to consumers.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility and Viability
1. Technical Feasibility: The platform can be developed using easily available technologies such as Python, Django, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and MySQL.

2. Economic Feasibility: The project requires low initial investment and uses affordable or open-source technologies, making it suitable for small-scale farmers.

3. Operational Feasibility: Farmers and consumers can easily use the platform through smartphones or computers with a simple and user-friendly interface.

4. Market Viability: There is a growing demand for fresh agricultural products and direct online purchasing, creating opportunities for this platform.

5. Scalability: The platform can be expanded to support more farmers, consumers, agricultural products and different regions.

6. Social Impact: It helps improve farmers' income, reduces unnecessary intermediaries and provides affordable products to consumers.

## Impact and Benefits
Impact and Benefits
1. Increased Farmer Income: Farmers can sell their products directly to buyers and earn better profits.

2. Reduced Consumer Prices: Eliminating unnecessary intermediaries helps reduce additional costs for consumers.

3. Direct Market Access: Farmers can reach more customers without depending on multiple agents and wholesalers.

4. Fresh Agricultural Products: Consumers can purchase fresh fruits, vegetables and grains directly from farmers.

5. Reduced Food Wastage: Faster communication between farmers and buyers helps reduce the wastage of agricultural products.

6. Price Transparency: AI-based price suggestions and live market updates help maintain fair pricing.

7. Employment Opportunities: Creates opportunities for local delivery partners and agricultural service providers.

8. Digital Empowerment: Encourages farmers to use digital technology for selling and managing their businesses.

Overall Impact: FarmDirect creates a transparent and sustainable agricultural marketplace that improves farmers' earnings, reduces consumer expenses and supports the growth of the agricultural sector.
